About us

Welcome to Hungry Hub

Hungry Hub started with a simple idea — serve fast food that makes everyone happy. We're a short walk from ICST University Park, built for students and staff who need something good to eat without losing their afternoon.

Every order is made when you ask for it, not stacked up hours in advance. Same fair prices, same short wait, every day.

About our restaurant

A small, busy kitchen right on ICST University Park friendly service and a menu built around what students actually order.

Our mission

Serve high quality food made from fresh ingredients at affordable prices, so good food brings people together.

About our food

We use the best ingredients we can source locally and follow strict quality standards, so every bite is tasty and safe.
